# Nock

Nock (usenock.com) is a Brazilian B2B prospecting platform. The `nock` MCP server
works on the signed-in user's Nock workspace:

- Search Brazilian companies (industry, size, location, CNAE, opening date) and the
  people who decide there.
- Look for buying signals: hiring, funding, new locations, technologies, news.
- Enrich work e-mails and mobiles, and keep results in sheets.
- Draft outreach campaigns. Nothing is sent until the user reviews and activates the
  campaign in Nock.

Data tools spend the workspace's credits, as in the dashboard. Say how many records
you plan to fetch before a large search or enrichment.
